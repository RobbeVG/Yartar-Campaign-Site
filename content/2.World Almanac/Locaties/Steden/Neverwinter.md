**Neverwinter**, also known as the **City of Skilled Hands** and the **Jewel of the North**, was a bustling, cultured, and cosmopolitan city-state in [northwest Faerûn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Northwest_Faer%C3%BBn "Northwest Faerûn"). Neverwinter was regarded by [Volo](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Volo "Volo") as the most cosmopolitan and civilized city in all of [[Faerûn]]. The city was a member in good standing of the [Lords' Alliance](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Lords%27_Alliance "Lords' Alliance").Known for its craftsfolk and gardeners, the city's multi-colored-glass lamps, precision water clocks, exquisite jewelry, and magnificent gardens ensured the warm winters were colorful and the summers were rich with fresh fruit.

>[!quote]+
> _Neverwinter is a friendly city of craftsmen, who trade extensively via the great merchants of Waterdeep; their water-clocks and multi-hued lamps can be found throughout the Realms. Neverwinter gained its name from the skill of its gardeners, who contrived to keep flowers blooming throughout the months of snow—a practice they continue with pride._
>  [Elminster](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elminster "Elminster")
