# Circlet of Blasting
*Wondrous item, uncommon*  
![](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5etools-mirror-3/5etools-img/main/items/XDMG/Circlet%20of%20Blasting.webp#right)


While wearing this circlet, you can cast [Scorching Ray](3.Mechanics/spells/scorching-ray-xphb.md) with it (`dice:1d20+5|noform|noparens|text(+5)` to hit). The circlet can't cast this spell again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide (2024) p. 244. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.2)'>SRD</span> and the Free Rules (2024)*
